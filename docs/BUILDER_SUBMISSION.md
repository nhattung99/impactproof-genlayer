## Problem and originality

NGOs, environmental initiatives, and grant programs often unlock funding through claims that funders cannot independently assess. ImpactProof freezes measurable criteria before evidence submission and makes the chain's interpretation inspectable. Its original workflow combines target/baseline context, credibility and contradiction analysis, conservative needs-review outcomes, evidence integrity metadata, and recorded funding eligibility.

## GenLayer fit

The Intelligent Contract retrieves public reports and performs subjective assessment inside GenLayer. Validators compare substantive reasoning and require agreement on the actual decision. A separate exact decision vote prevents an opposite enum from passing through semantic comparison. No frontend/server AI verdict generator exists.

## Demonstration sequence

1. Explain a real authorized project, baseline, target, and methodology.
2. Connect the creator wallet on stable studionet.
3. Register a project, define a milestone, and show transaction finality.
4. Submit 1–3 actual public textual sources.
5. Request verification and show pending consensus separately from finalized state.
6. Inspect the final decision, evidence availability, reasoning, limitations, methodology, and eligibility.
7. Refresh and show the same finalized record.
8. Show that approval does not claim a fund transfer.
